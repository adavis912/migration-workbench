# HR-CORE - HR Core (COTS HCM)

Last updated: 2026-10-05

Interview with Dana Castell, HR systems manager.

This is the vendor HCM product. The app servers are on RHEL 6 because that is the last version the vendor certified for our release. The vendor has not confirmed support for a newer OS. Open enrollment and year-end payroll mean no changes December 1 through January 15.
